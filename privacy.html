Privacy Policy

Project SMART-ANHS, Alcantara National High School. Last updated November 23, 2026.

Project SMART-ANHS is a school information website. This page explains what information the site handles, where it is stored, and who can see it.

Information we handle

  • Account details. School staff who sign in give an email address and a password. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase, and are not visible to us.
  • School records and content. Information that authorized school staff enter into the site, such as announcements, calendars, resources, and reports.
  • Uploaded files. Documents (for example PDFs, scans, and reports) that authorized staff upload through the site.
  • Activity records. A log of actions such as uploads, downloads, and deletions, with the account email that performed them, to keep the system accountable.

Visitors who only browse the public pages are not asked to sign in and do not give us personal information.

How Google Drive is used

Uploaded files are stored in a folder on the school's Google Drive account. The site connects to Google Drive through a secure server function, using the narrow drive.file permission. With this permission the site can only create, read, and manage the files and the folder that it created itself. It cannot see any other file in the Drive account.

The site does not ask visitors or staff to sign in with their own Google accounts, and it does not collect, read, or store Google user data belonging to them.

How information is used

Information is used only to run the school website: to let authorized staff sign in, manage content, and store and retrieve school documents. We do not sell your information. We do not use it for advertising. We do not share it with third parties, except the service providers that host the site (Supabase for sign-in and database, Google Drive for file storage) and only as needed to provide those services.

Who can see it

Access depends on the role given to each account. Only signed-in users with the right role can upload, download, or delete files. Only administrators and the school head can delete files.

Retention and deletion

Records and files are kept for as long as the school needs them. Deleted files are moved to the Google Drive trash, where they can be recovered for 30 days before Google removes them permanently. To ask for your information to be corrected or removed, contact us using the details below.

Data privacy law

We handle personal information in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and applicable DepEd policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
